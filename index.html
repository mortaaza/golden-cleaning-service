About Golden Cleaning Service

Golden Cleaning Service is a family-run cleaning company. We started by cleaning for friends and neighbours, and grew through word of mouth. Today we look after homes, rental properties and offices across the region.

We believe great cleaning is about trust. That is why the same trained cleaner comes back to your home, why we are fully insured, and why we never charge for a visit you are not happy with.
